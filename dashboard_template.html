Emerald Park

A ballpark designed to help hitters (more home runs, more power) and pitchers (fewer hits on balls in play, more foul-ball outs) at the same time, while the run environment stays about neutral.

Why it is both pitcher- and batter-friendly

Short pull-side alleys and a deep center field move home runs toward pull-power. Huge foul territory and a slow, lush grass infield take away hits.

How it works and what to trust

Real data: 2019 batting lines (Lahman Baseball Databank): each hitter's strikeout, walk and HBP rates are used as-is, and his home-run rate is matched exactly by fitting a power parameter. Park-run factors come from the real 2019 park factors for validation (model vs. Lahman park factor correlation across 30 parks: r = ). Modeled: exit velocity, launch angle and spray angle (the Lahman data has no Statcast), flight distance from a drag-adjusted projectile model, fielding and hit probabilities. Real park dimensions are approximate public figures. Treat the exact percentages as model estimates, not predictions.
